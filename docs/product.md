 2. Introduction 

 Victoria Eagles Brand, based in Kisumu, Kenya, is a digital marketing and technology
company committed to driving innovation through digital transformation and artificial
intelligence. Eagles Construct AI emerges from the need to address inefficiencies in
construction management, bringing a data-driven, intelligent, and collaborative approach
to the sector.

3. Problem Statement

The construction industry faces major challenges including material wastage, poor
coordination, lack of accountability, and inefficient resource utilization. Traditional methods
often lead to project delays, inflated costs, and low productivity.

4. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of Eagles Construct AI are to optimize resource usage, reduce
material wastage, enhance workforce efficiency, and foster seamless collaboration among
construction stakeholders.

5. Solution Overview-Eagles Construc AI

Eagles Construct AI provides a centralized digital platform powered by AI analytics to
manage and monitor construction processes in real-time. It automates data collection,
analyzes material consumption, and provides insights for better decision-making.
